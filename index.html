The Displacement of Native Hawaiians Due to Predatory Tourism

Introduction

Many people dream of a Hawaiian vacation, viewing it as a sort of paradise, but this is far from the reality for the people who call Hawaii their home. Ever since Captain Cook "discovered" the islands of Hawaii in 1778, people from around the world felt entitled to Hawaii's beauty. This is still true today, with around 9 to 10 million people visiting Hawaii each year and developers buying up land for resorts and airbnbs. While these millions of people enjoy the islands, native Hawaiians struggle with the increasing cost of living, the lack of housing, and being the last priority when a natural disaster strikes, causing many of them to move to the U.S while rich people take their homes.
